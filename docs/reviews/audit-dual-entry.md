# Audit: dual entry and derivable facts (Colorado volume)

Read-only audit, 2026-11-21, branch `ccr-9d19bf4c-wmnwcl` at 57663b1 (working tree as another agent was editing it). Scope: the OQ-97 / OQ-95 class of problem, under the product-owner decisions at the end of docs/plan-colorado.md, which are binding here:

- "Supplied facts are program-neutral statements of what is true of the person (one immigration status, one income receipt, one relationship). Each program's meaning of a shared word ... lives in that program's own derived items, never in a second supplied fact."
- "Facts that another record or edge can supply are read from it, not entered twice (OQ-97, OQ-95). Where a rule asks for a lighter determination (attested rather than computed income, OQ-54), the ledger uses the lighter fact."
- "OQ-44 is to be fixed: one immigration-status fact with the full documented category set; per-program derived eligibility."

## Method

`npm run export -- --volume co` (1,396 items: 517 supplied, 599 derived, 280 parameters) was read item by item: every supplied fact's meaning and readers, and the tree of every derived item that reads one of the facts below. The 26 household cases in `volumes/co/tests/cases.yaml` were parsed and checked for derived values stated as facts, stale identifiers, facts no item reads, and pairs of facts that disagree. Rule-test mentions were counted from `conformance/co/suite.json` (2,236 rule tests). `npm run check -- --volume co` was run for its case warnings. Scripts are in /tmp/claude-0/audits/.

Impact figures in the tables read "rt / hc / ps": rule tests that state the fact, household cases that state it, and person statements across those cases.

docs/reviews/vocabulary-reuse.md (D-6, R-15, C-5, C-6, U-16) declined several of these items as "different facts, not copies" or "no case-scope fact yet". The product-owner decision now governs those choices, so they are re-raised here where the record or edge exists; items that review fixed are not repeated.

Priorities: **P1** affects an eligibility or benefit answer or certification (the case data already disagree, a rule reads a copy nobody maintains, or two programs read different copies of one fact); **P2** maintainability or approval risk (the copies agree today); **P3** style or dead data.

## 3. Sets of supplied facts that state one real-world fact

### 3.1 Immigration status (OQ-44): one neutral fact and per-program derived items (P1)

**Problem.** One fact (the person's immigration status, as USCIS records it and SAVE returns it) is spread over four supplied facts that can disagree:

- `citizenship_status` (CO-002): ten options, one of which (`other_qualified_non_citizen`) lumps four qualified categories, parolees for a year or more, deportation withheld, conditional entrants and battered non-citizens (CO-74 and CO-75 record a parolee that way), and one of which (`other_lawfully_present_non_citizen`) lumps every lawfully residing category;
- `lawful_presence_status` (CO-084): `lawfully_present` or `not_lawfully_present`. It restates the CO-002 option `other_lawfully_present_non_citizen` and is the only fact `ma_is_lawfully_present` (CO-507) reads for that group;
- `federal_five_year_bar_exempt_category` (CO-1724): `refugee_or_asylee_adjusted_to_lpr`, `deportation_withheld`, `amerasian_immigrant`. `deportation_withheld` is itself a qualified status (8 U.S.C. 1641(b)(5)), so it must agree with CO-002 = `other_qualified_non_citizen`. The other two presuppose CO-002 = `lawful_permanent_resident`;
- `ma_five_year_bar_exempt_category` (CO-1170): four more status-history categories (Canadian-born American Indian, Hmong or Highland Lao veteran, Iraqi or Afghan special immigrant adjusted to LPR, trafficking victim).

What goes wrong today:

1. A child or pregnant woman with `citizenship_status: other_lawfully_present_non_citizen` and `lawful_presence_status` unstated gets `ma_is_lawfully_present` = unknown (the `any` of false, false and unknown). `ma_meets_citizenship_requirement` (CO-509, 17 readers) then makes the determination *cannot determine*.
2. `citizenship_status: undocumented` with `lawful_presence_status: lawfully_present` is accepted as lawfully residing.
3. No case states `other_lawfully_present_non_citizen`. Every lawfully present non-qualified case is carried by CO-084 alone, so CCCAP's CO-998, which reads only CO-002, and Medicaid's CO-507, which reads only CO-084, can reach opposite answers for the same child.
4. `lawful_presence_status` is stated beside `citizenship_status` on 48 persons in 14 cases, always consistently, which is the dual entry itself.
5. Medicaid's ii category ("a lawfully residing non-citizen who entered the United States prior to August 22, 1996") and viii (Afghan humanitarian parolees) are not reachable, because neither fact can carry them.

**Governing text.** 10 CCR 2505-10 § 8.100.3.G.1.g.i-viii (D-65; excerpts S174, S656, S657, S658), 8 U.S.C. 1641(b)-(c), 1612 and 1613 (S173, S176, S1150), 7 U.S.C. 2015(f) as amended (S8, S9), 9 CCR 2503-6 3.604.1.B and C.R.S. 26-2-111.8 (S402, S1151), 9 CCR 2503-7 3.753.16-17 (S493, S494), 8 CCR 1403-1 3.111.F.1 (S514), and 42 U.S.C. 1396b(v)(5) (S1700, S1702).
