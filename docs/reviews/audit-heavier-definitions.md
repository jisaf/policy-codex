# Audit: heavier-than-needed definitions (the OQ-54 class)

Read-only audit of the Colorado volume at commit 57663b1 (branch ccr-9d19bf4c-wmnwcl, clean tree; `npm run export -- --volume co`, 1,396 items). Binding context: the product-owner decisions in docs/plan-colorado.md, in particular "Where a rule asks for a lighter determination (attested rather than computed income, OQ-54), the ledger uses the lighter fact", and conventions.md rule 11 ("Receipt of another program's benefit is a supplied fact about the person's case ... never this volume's own determination of that program").

## Scope and method

"Heavier" means more burden than the governing text asks for: more facts, verification where attestation suffices, a computed figure where a declared or recorded one is allowed, a full household where the rule looks at one person, a precise amount where a yes/no is enough, a document where a data match or attestation is allowed. The opposite direction (a lighter fact than the rule requires) is reported separately and is P1 throughout, as the brief directs.

For every determination type the brief names (presumptive eligibility, Express Lane, expedited SNAP, SNAP categorical eligibility and its verification relief, renewal and recertification, continuous eligibility and the pregnancy lock-in, emergency Medicaid, LEAP, SUN Bucks streamlined certification, CCCAP self-declaration, Adult Financial, Colorado Works) and for every `*_requires_verification_of_*` and `*_verification_is_pending` item, I compared the item's tree, and the supplied leaves it reaches through `uses`, with the cited excerpt in volumes/co/sources.md. I also listed every derived item that reads another program's derived outcome (14 reads, the `accepts_from` set) and tested each against rule 11. Items I checked and found consistent are listed at the end so the fix round does not re-audit them.

Limits: the web fetcher was out of credit, so every finding rests on the excerpts already in sources.md. Where an item's meaning quotes rule text its cited excerpt does not contain, that is called out (H-8). OQ-54, OQ-56, OQ-308 and OQ-311 (BCE-only verification relief) are resolved and were not reopened. docs/reviews/vocabulary-reuse.md fixed U-16 by writing convention rule 11; H-1 to H-4 are the places that rule was not applied.
