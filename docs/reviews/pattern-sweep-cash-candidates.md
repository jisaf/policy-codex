## Verdicts

- **R-DATED**: Right: a dated fact (date_of_birth, date_of_death, institution_entry_date, the pregnancy dates, the application, eligibility and approval dates) read against the month evaluated, or a record read by its month, date or start and end (received_in, P57).
- **R-AGE**: Right: the age is taken in the month evaluated (years_between to the month's first or last day; OQ-951 for the day).
- **R-PROCESS**: Right: a fact of the determination itself (a verification document or request, an interview, a due date, an attestation), one value for the determination: 9 CCR 2503-5 3.520.4-3.520.5 and 9 CCR 2503-6 3.604.3 verify at the application or redetermination.
- **R-HCA**: Right: Home Care Allowance eligibility is decided at the approval and paid only from the month after it, with no retroactive payment (9 CCR 2503-5 3.570.13.F, S3503), so the determination's facts decide every month paid.
- **R-REDET**: Right: 9 CCR 2503-6 3.605.5.C tests child support at the redetermination, over the six months before it (S2607).
- **R-UNIT**: Right: a bar the rule lays on the whole unit for one member of it (closures, diversion period, sanctions, child support cooperation, strike basis), read over the determination's members (coverage pass 6 CA6-4); the unit's size, income, standards and basis are the month's (CO-7304).
- **R-COUNTY**: Right: a finding the rule has the county (or a hearing) establish, so none stated is none: IPV and EBT findings (9 CCR 2503-5 3.520.61.H; 9 CCR 2503-6 3.609.5), failures to apply or cooperate (3.520.71.D; 3.604.2.M.4, 3.604.4.C), refused potential income or resources (3.520.71.G-H; 3.605.1.A.3), rebuttals (3.520.64.E; 3.605.1.A.5.b), good cause and extensions (3.604.3.D), closures and sanctions (3.601 Sanction, 3.608.4), the diversion period (3.606.8), the AND-SO hardship and exception findings, the requested inclusion of an optional member (3.604.2.C.2: the member is in the unit only on request).
- **R-DECLARED**: Right: the fact's absence has a meaning declared on the fact (date_of_death: not stated for a living person; temporarily_out_of_state_start_date: no absence; a record field's when_absent; cw_unit_eligible_from_date: eligible on the date of application, 3.609.2.A.1; af_hca_approval_date: not approved before this determination, PS-D1; the shared verification request date as the program's own, one statement of one date).
- **R-RECORD-RULE**: Right: the record field's default is the rule's: income and resources count unless an exclusion or deduction is shown (9 CCR 2503-6 3.605.3: "Unless otherwise specified, any unearned income is countable"; 3.605.1.A; 9 CCR 2503-5 3.520.781.C and 3.520.786: "Such exempt income is limited to the following"; 3.520.72.A: "Unless otherwise specified, a resource is countable"), and a payment is the month's unless shown otherwise (3.520.781.G; 3.605.1.A.2): an earmark, set-aside, foster-care, farm, irregular, retroactive or temporary flag defaults to the reading that counts the payment in its month.
- **R-RECORD-TIME**: Right: the record is read in its month: its month or date (P57), and a stream only from its start or its job's start through its end (CA4-9, FS-AF-1, FS-CW-1); an absent month, start or end has the meaning the schema declares (when_absent).
- **R-BOUND**: Right: a sound lower bound, not a default: af_meets_resource_limit answers false when the parts known already exceed the limit, every unknown part being 0 or more (CO-5350, H1).
- **R-STRUCT**: Right: the composition is built per month from the month's members (CO-7303, CO-7304: born by the month's end, alive on its first day, a dependent child, parent, caretaker or expectant parent of the month, and since PS-T8 living in the home in the month); the person-scope groups answer who may join, as in coverage pass 6.
- **R-STATIC**: Right: a fact that does not change within the months a determination covers (a relationship's kind, a citizenship document, a table key, a stated policy choice of the case).
- **R-DIVERSION**: Right: a diversion is assessed and agreed at the determination, for a one-time payment (9 CCR 2503-6 3.606.8.A), so its facts are the determination's.
- **R-DISASTER**: Right: disaster assistance is a payment determined on the client's request (9 CCR 2503-5 3.570.51-52), from the determination's facts.
- **R-CURRENT**: Right: the text names the current figure: the transfer penalty divides by "the current Adult Financial Program grant standard" (3.520.76.D.2, S2614).
- **R-AVERAGE**: Right: irregular self-employment is averaged over a twelve-month period (3.520.783.F), the months read by sum_months; the undated sum only asks whether such a record exists.
