# Three-pattern sweep: SNAP, CCCAP, LEAP and SUN Bucks (family `pattern-sweep-snap-ce`), from 414d239

A systematic sweep, 2026-11-24, of every item of SNAP (volumes/co/snap), CCCAP (childcare), LEAP (energy) and SUN Bucks (sunbucks), and of the shared items and parameters they read, for the three patterns that six rounds of coverage reviews kept finding (docs/reviews/coverage6-snap.md, coverage6-care-energy.md; coverage4-snap.md, coverage4-care-energy.md, coverage5-care-energy.md):

1. **Month-by-month timing.** An item evaluated for a month reads the facts and ages of that month (docs/plan-colorado.md, the `month-v1` contract; docs/operators.md, "Ages in a month"), unless the rule text fixes the reading at a point in time.
2. **Defaults the rules don't give.** Every `otherwise` fallback, every record field `default` and `when_absent`, every `lives_with` and `dwelling_id` fallback (docs/conventions.md rule 17) is either a default a cited rule gives, declared on the fact, or required where read (decision 4).
3. **Couples and household membership.** Who is in the household or unit in each month, and whether every count and sum uses the right membership.

Branch `pattern-sweep-snap-ce` from `ccr-9d19bf4c-wmnwcl` at 414d239. Concurrent rounds: the same sweep for non-MAGI Medicaid and for MAGI/CHP+, and a cash fix round; this round edits only SNAP, CCCAP, LEAP and SUN Bucks items and tables, plus the neutral shared items named in section 3 (documented in docs/conventions.md rules 17 and 18).

## 1. Method

- **Candidates, mechanically.** `scripts/audit/pattern-sweep.ts` loads the volume, takes every item of the four programs and, transitively, every shared item and parameter they read, and lists:
  - P1: inside a month-scoped item (`person-month`, `month`), every read of a person- or case-scope derived item (one value per case, on the determination date) and of a dated or status supplied fact; every `det_date`, `det_month` and `at`; every record sum whose condition reads none of a stream's source dates (`start_date`, `end_date`, `job_start_date`, `job_end_date`).
  - P2: every `otherwise` and its default; every record field with a `default` or `when_absent` that a program item reads; every `lives_with` and `dwelling_id` read.
  - P3: every item that builds or reads a household, unit or member group, with the composition facts it reads.
  At 414d239 it lists **535 P1, 310 P2 and 220 P3 candidates** (1,065 distinct; a candidate is one item and one read, and a read repeated within an item is listed once). `--root` reads an exported commit; `--verdicts scripts/audit/pattern-sweep-snap-ce-verdicts.yaml` groups every candidate under the first verdict rule it matches and lists any candidate no rule matches. At 414d239: **0 unmatched**.
- **Judgement.** Each verdict class was judged against the fetched rule text (volumes/co/documents/; excerpts in volumes/co/sources.md), and every class whose reading is wrong became a fix. Hand reading also covered what the mechanical list cannot see: dated supplied facts whose names the status pattern misses (`received_energy_assistance_within_twelve_months`, T-10), a person's date of death or birth that no SNAP, CCCAP or LEAP household read (T-7, T-8), and a status a dated fact ends (graduation, T-11).
- **Probes.** Each defect has a probe household computed by hand from the rule text; every probe is a rule test of the changed item (and two are stored households, CO-940 and CO-941).
