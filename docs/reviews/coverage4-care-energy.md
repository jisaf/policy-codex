## 1. Verdict

**9 P1 found** (4 LEAP, 5 CCCAP; none in SUN Bucks). They cluster in the new income records: how dated pays and dated receipts are placed in each program's income period, a gap in the records' shape (two jobs on one pay schedule), a default the records keep against decision 4, and three CCCAP paragraphs the records now make reachable but no item reads (H.4.b contract pay, I.32 VISTA, the per-activity minimum-wage test). One LEAP P1 is a point-in-time slip of the time contract (the child's age on the determination date, not the application date).

- **L4-1** LEAP averages two jobs paid on the same schedule together when both are stated as dated pays, counting one job's pay.
- **L4-2** A dated pay that states no frequency is read as monthly (the record's default), not cannot determine; OQ-1181 records a codex default as a state policy choice.
- **L4-3** LEAP reads recurring unearned receipts by the calendar month of the application, so a dated receipt after the application date is counted and one in the income verification period before the application month is not (3.752.22.A, C.3).
- **L4-4** LEAP tests a child's age (3.751.2.D.2 membership, 3.752.23.Q exclusion) on the determination date, not the application date 3.752.2 fixes.
- **C4-1** CCCAP's J.28 test reads the calendar quarter of the month determined, not the quarter of a dated irregular pay drawn from the thirty days.
- **C4-2** CCCAP reads dated unearned receipts by calendar month, not in the thirty days OQ-680's assumption fixes (dated pays are read in the thirty days).
- **C4-3** CCCAP's minimum-wage test divides wages plus self-employment by all hours, so a break-even business disqualifies a caretaker whose job alone meets 3.111.G.1.
- **C4-4** CCCAP does not read annual contract pay (3.111.H.4.b, "shall be prorated"), although the earnings record carries it.
- **C4-5** CCCAP has no element for 3.111.I.32's VISTA rule (excluded for a client receiving CCCAP when she joined), and no kind for VISTA pay at or above the minimum wage.

There are also 8 P2 and 1 P3. SUN Bucks has no P1: its income test reads the application's reported income, and its receipt table only decides the TANF categorical ground. Every earlier P1 still holds (section 5). Four open questions are misclassified or carry stale assumptions (section 4).
