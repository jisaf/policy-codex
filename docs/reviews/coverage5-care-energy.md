## 1. Verdict

**9 P1 found** (4 LEAP, 2 CCCAP, 1 SUN Bucks, 1 in both CCCAP and LEAP, 1 LEAP point-in-time). Pass 4's nine fixes are each right on the households they were written for (section 5); what they missed is the same class reached through fields that pass 4 did not read, or that other passes added to the shared records after pass 4 began.

- **C5-1** CCCAP counts a stream (an earnings, business or receipt record with no date) in the month determined even when the record says its job, business or source ended before the thirty days: `job_end_date`, `end_date` (and `job_start_date`, `start_date`) are read by no CCCAP item. The cash and SNAP families fixed this class for their own readers; CCCAP did not.
- **C5-2** CCCAP deducts the early-withdrawal penalty (3.111.I.8) only from a recurring `pension` receipt, so it never reaches an early 401(k) or IRA withdrawal, which the record schema states as `savings_account_distribution`, nor any withdrawal paid once.
- **L5-1** LEAP counts a receipt stream whose `end_date` is before the income verification period or whose `start_date` is after the application date (3.752.22.C.3.a, C.3.c), and a self-employment stream in "the full month prior" when the business began after it or ended before it (3.755.21.I.1).
- **L5-2** LEAP counts the part of a military allotment or VA benefit paid for a person outside the budget unit (3.752.212 "for those individuals included in the budget unit"; 3.752.211.E "countable unearned income to the dependent rather than the veteran"): `for_person_living_elsewhere` and `for_dependent` are read by no LEAP item.
- **L5-3** LEAP excludes every retroactive one-time receipt; 3.752.23.EE excludes only "retroactive lump-sum social security, SSI, and public assistance payments".
- **L5-4** LEAP counts cash value taken from the person's own insurance policy (`insurance_cash_value`, "counted because no rule excludes it") although 3.752.23.GG excludes "Money obtained from the liquidation of personal resources"; the same money stated as `resource_liquidation` is excluded.
- **L5-5** LEAP reads the point-in-time factors other than age (dwelling type, homelessness, alien status) from facts stated "on the determination date" (CO-2810, CO-002), not as of the application date 3.752.2 and 3.751.1 "Point in Time" fix: the class of pass 4's L4-4, fixed for age only.
- **S5-1** SUN Bucks reads a Colorado Works cash stream as received in every month of the period of eligibility even when its `end_date` is before the period, so a child whose household left TANF before July 1 is categorically eligible.
- **X5-1** CCCAP (3.111.H.4.e) and LEAP (3.752.21.F) count income from jointly owned property "in a percentage at least equal to the percentage of ownership": the ownership percentage, and the property's income, are an element no item or record field defines, so a co-owner who receives less than her share is under-counted.

There are also 6 P2 and 2 P3. Section 4 lists two open questions whose signed text no longer covers what the engine does and two readings that need an open question.
