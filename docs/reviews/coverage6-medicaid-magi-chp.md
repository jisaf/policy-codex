## 1. Verdict

**7 P1 (3 common, 4 rare), 3 P2, 1 P3. The family does not meet the stopping rule.**

- **C6-01 (common).** The Pregnant Women coverage period opens only on the application month (or a requested retroactive month). A woman enrolled before her pregnancy (the usual Health First Colorado member who becomes pregnant) has an application month long before it, so no period opens even when the eligibility history records `pregnant` for every month of the pregnancy: her 12 postpartum months are lost whenever her income is over the Adults standard after the birth.
- **C6-02 (rare).** The same anchor means a woman not eligible in the application month but eligible as a pregnant woman in a later month of the same application (Medicaid G.5; CHP+ § 430.3, whose text names "the first day of the month the pregnant woman becomes eligible") gets no postpartum period or enrollment span.
- **C6-03 (common shape).** A recorded birth does not end the pregnancy: with the baby in the case (gave_birth_to, date of birth) but no separate pregnancy_end_date, the mother is read as not pregnant in the birth month and without a postpartum period (CO-089's stated default "not having been pregnant"), and a stated pregnancy start with no end is read the same way.
- **C6-04 (common shape, decision 4).** An unanswered "will anyone claim you as a tax dependent" (CO-2716) is read as "no", so a young adult whose parents claim her is determined on her own income.
- **C6-05 (rare, decision 4).** Four more reader defaults give a definite answer on a missing fact: an unstated state of residence is `ineligible_residency`; an unstated needy-person basis is "none"; an unstated application date makes every past month a covered month; an unstated amount of support from a sponsor found indigent deems nothing.
- **C6-06 (rare).** A pregnancy whose stated start date is after the application month is read as a pregnancy in the application month (CO-3601, CO-7051), so that month is granted under Pregnant Women.
- **C6-07 (rare; OQ-810 fails its class check in part).** C.4.a annualizes the whole income of a member with self-employment, seasonal or commission earnings, so a lump sum received in the month is spread over the year, against C.4.a's named incomes and C.1.d.i.

The pass-5 fixes hold for the households they were written for (section 3); C6-01 to C6-03 are gaps beside C5-01 and C5-02, and C6-04 is the half of C5-05 the field sweep recorded without fixing.
