## SNAP

### KL-SNAP-1 (coverage pass 7, C7-6; OQ-1450): a status stated for the determination date is read in an earlier application month

- **Description.** The case states some statuses once, for the determination date (the nighttime residence other than a stay in another's home, a residence setting left between the application and the determination, the receipt of SSI, TANF or State cash assistance where a reader reads the award, immigration and disability statuses), and SNAP reads them in an application month before the determination month. Two persons joined by a household edge who live apart on the determination date with no separation date stated are read apart in the determination month (a separation inside the determination month is read on OQ-1405's day only when its date is stated).
- **Households affected.** An application in one month determined in the next (ordinary) whose status changed in between (uncommon): a member discharged from a hospital, a newly approved SSI award, a family that left a shelter.
- **Common / rare.** Rare.
- **Rule paragraph.** 7 CFR 273.10(a)(1)(i) (S5091): the application month on "the household's circumstances for the entire month of application"; 10 CCR 2506-1 4.402.1.A (S4734).
- **Household that shows it.** Rule test CO-7672-T5: spouses apart on 2026-11-05 with no separation date are apart in November, the determination month (a husband who moved out on November 2 states the date and is a member of November, CO-7672-T2, stored household CO-1070). A status example: CO-1073 with the family in a shelter instead of a friend's home on the determination date and in its own home in October would read October as homeless; only a stay in another person's home states the residence before it (CO-7674).
- **Proposed fix.** Date each status SNAP reads for an application month (the residence's start for every temporary accommodation, the award dates of SSI, TANF and State cash, the discharge date from an institution), required where read for a month before the determination month, as coverage pass 7 dated who lives in the home (CO-7670 to CO-7672), the energy assistance payments (CO-7673) and a stay in another's home (CO-7674). Until then OQ-1450 records the reading for the SNAP owner.

### KL-SNAP-4 (close-out verification F2; V7-2's neighbour): the part of a VA benefit for a dependent who lives in the home but is not a member of the SNAP household is counted

**Closed (commit a6266c4).** SNAP membership reads income only through the elderly and disabled separate household test (CO-2304, CO-419: the 165 percent test of the others' income); CO-305's other parts (the homes of the month, the spouse, mandatory parent and child and caretaker edges, purchase and prepare, institution, boarder, death and birth) read none. snap_household_members_before_elderly_disabled_separation (CO-7892, CO-305's sibling) is CO-305 without that test, and CO-5011 takes the for_dependent part off where for_person is outside it: a dependent who lives elsewhere, or who lives in the home and buys and prepares food apart and is no mandatory member (a parent of the veteran; a child of 22 or older). `npm run check` finds no dependency cycle. One case is left to the SNAP owner (OQ-1510): a dependent who buys and prepares with the household and could be granted separate household status as a person sixty or older with a disability; her membership turns on the others' income, which includes this payment, and the part is counted with the household as before. Rule tests CO-7892-T1 to T4, CO-5011-T24 to T27; stored household CO-1183 (probe H2: 900 counted, $320; was $185). The entry as recorded:

- **Description.** 10 CCR 2506-1 4.405.B excludes "Monies received and used for the care and maintenance of a third-party beneficiary who is not a household member". SNAP's counted recurring receipts (snap_counted_recurring_receipts, CO-5011) take the dependent's part (for_dependent) off a receipt only where the dependent (for_person) does not live with the person (V7-2), not where the dependent lives in the home but is not a member of the SNAP household (CO-305), such as a parent of 60 or older who buys and prepares food separately, or a child of 22 or older who is a separate household. CO-5011 reads co-residence rather than SNAP membership because the household of the month (CO-305) reads income through the 165% elderly-disabled separate-household test, so reading membership here would be a circular read.
- **Households affected.** A SNAP household with a VA (or other) benefit that includes a part for a dependent who lives in the home and is not a household member.
- **Common / rare.** Rare.
- **Rule paragraph.** 10 CCR 2506-1 4.405.B (S3100): "Monies received and used for the care and maintenance of a third-party beneficiary who is not a household member. If the intended beneficiaries of a single payment are both household and non-household members, any identifiable portion of the payment intended and used for the care and maintenance of the non-household member shall be excluded."
- **Household that shows it.** Probe H2 of docs/reviews/verify-closeout-fixes.md: CO-1165 with the dependent the veteran's mother, p5, 76, living in the home and buying and preparing separately (snap_household_members {p1, p2, p3}). - **Proposed fix.** CO-5011 takes the dependent's part off where for_person is not a member of the person's SNAP household in the month (snap_household_members, CO-305), not only where for_person does not live with the person, once the read is shown not to cycle through the 165% elderly-disabled separate-household test (or reads a membership figure that test does not read); a rule test and a stored household from probe H2.

### KL-CE-1 (coverage pass 7, R7-3; OQ-1365, OQ-1366): one rental property cannot be stated so that CCCAP and LEAP both read its costs right

**Closed (commit 048c94b).** The restatement the entry proposes is made. A business's receipts record and each of its cost records name the business (`job`, earnings CO-5100), and a cost of rental property says whether it is the property's maintenance (`property_maintenance`, a new neutral field, required where read). CCCAP takes a rental managed under twenty hours a week net of its own cost records (cccap_rental_property_costs, CO-7910, through cccap_excluded_unearned_receipts CO-4463; operating costs and mortgage interest, not principal or depreciation, OQ-1515) and leaves them out of the self-employment costs (CO-1709); `rental_property_expenses_paid` (CO-4458) is taken against `rental_income` receipts only. Both statement rules are withdrawn, and the earnings translator keeps `job` and `property_maintenance` as the household states them (scripts/records/translate-earnings.ts, `KEPT_BUSINESS_FIELDS`, `KEPT_COST_FIELDS`). A cost's `job` is read only for a person with rental property, and `property_maintenance` only on a cost of rental property (by LEAP). Stored households, each stating the property once: CO-1082 (CCCAP $3,100, eligible_low_income; LEAP $3,300, eligible), CO-1083 (CCCAP $3,700, ineligible_income; LEAP $3,700, eligible), CO-1190 (probe CR2: CCCAP $3,100, eligible_low_income, was $3,400 ineligible_income; LEAP $3,100, eligible), CO-1191 (probe LRB1: LEAP $3,900, not eligible, was $3,300 eligible), CO-1192 (probe LRB3: LEAP $3,700, eligible, was $3,800 not eligible); CO-870, CO-1080 and CO-1081 state their rentals' costs as cost records, answers unchanged. Rule tests CO-7910-T1 to T6, CO-1709-T9 to T12, CO-1711-T7 to T13, CO-1712-T7, CO-4463-T15 to T17. The entry as recorded:

- **Description.** CCCAP takes the costs of a rental managed under twenty hours a week from `rental_property_expenses_paid` (CO-4458, through cccap_excluded_unearned_receipts CO-4463 and cccap_rental_property_receipts_as_unearned CO-7251) and ignores the rental business's cost records; LEAP takes a rental business's costs from its cost records only (leap_self_employment_allowable_costs CO-1711, with a statement rule: maintenance as `business_cost_operating`, no interest or vehicle) and does not read the business's `rental_property`, so it deducts whatever cost records are stated. - **Households affected.** A landlord who manages a rental property under twenty hours a week and applies for CCCAP and LEAP together (one PEAK application), or a LEAP landlord whose rental business has mortgage interest, property taxes or insurance stated as cost records.
- **Common / rare.** Rare.
- **Rule paragraph.** 8 CCR 1403-1 3.111.H.4.e (S2704), I.12 and I.17 ("Net rental income"); 9 CCR 2503-7 3.755.21.I.2.D ("Rental property allowable expenses shall be limited to the costs of maintenance of the property", D-86).
- **Households that show it.** CO-1082 (stated the CCCAP way: the $300 of costs as rental_property_expenses_paid; CCCAP $3,100, eligible_low_income, which the rule gives) and CO-1083 (stated the LEAP way: $100 of maintenance as a business_cost_operating record; LEAP $3,700, eligible, which the rule gives). The other statements, as probes:

CCCAP reads a rental under the line net of its own business's cost records (and leaves them out of the self-employment costs, CO-1709), keeping rental_property_expenses_paid for a `rental_income` receipt; LEAP reads `rental_property` on the business and deducts only its maintenance records (I.2.D).
