## 2. Decisions taken and why

- **One Medicaid item, not CO-505.** is_colorado_resident (CO-505) is read by SNAP (CO-4253), CHP+ (CO-7402), Colorado Works (CO-904), Adult Financial (CO-942), CCCAP (CO-2107) and LEAP (CO-1007), each at the point in time its own rule fixes (the application, the certification, the month for Adult Financial's own residency item). Medicaid's month reading is its own item over CO-2250, so no other program's reader changes. CHP+ has no retroactive months (10 CCR 2505-3 § 430.1) and its application month is a month the applicant lived in Colorado, so CO-7402 is unchanged (OQ-1425).
- **The application month boundary.** B.1 has the person apply in the county where the person lives and B.2 defines residence "at the time of application", so the application month (any day, G.1; C.1) and the months after need no date; the date is read, and required, only for a month before the application month. B.2's words could also be read as fixing residence at the application for the retroactive months themselves; OQ-1445 records it, with 435.915(a)(2)'s reading as the assumption.
- **The date is required where read** (decision 4 of 2026-11-23), as the non-MAGI sweep made medicare_part_a_entitlement_date (CO-7502) and adult_financial_program_start_date (CO-7507). A stored household whose story has the person in Colorado in every month it covers states it (52 persons in 36 households, each line commented: the first day of the earliest month the household covers, or the birth if later; the story gives no earlier date), so every stored expectation is unchanged. The stories of CO-1060 and CO-1061 state a move.
- **Reuse, no second derivation.** CO-7550 to CO-7553 are the MAGI sweep's; the non-MAGI readers read them. The one change to a reused item is CO-7551's `case` for a month before the qualified status, which every Medicaid category needs, MAGI included.
- **Status before the qualified status.** qualified_status_date is the date of a change the case states, so OQ-1425's assumption (the stated fact holds) no longer reaches the months before it. What the person held then is stated only for a lawful permanent resident; for the others, lawful presence (which the case does not date) is read as stated and the qualified arms are not (OQ-1446).

## 3. Open questions

- **OQ-1445** (new; state policy choice, with Legal advising; Medicaid policy owner): § 8.100.3.B.2's "at the time of application" against 42 CFR 435.915(a)(2)'s "at the time" for a retroactive month's residence.
- **OQ-1446** (new; state policy choice; Medicaid policy owner): the status held in a retroactive month before a qualified status was granted, for a status other than permanent residence.
- **OQ-1425** narrowed (its body says what is now dated); the assumption still governs the facts no date states. IDs OQ-1447 to OQ-1449 unused.
