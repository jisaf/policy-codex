## 1. Verdict

**10 P1 found. SNAP's resource test does not meet the stopping rule.**

Every P1 bites only a household the resource test reaches (4.408.A: BCE and ECE households are exempt), so each is a rarer household than the income P1s of coverage4-snap.md; but each gives a household with a member serving an IPV disqualification, a drug-felony bar, or an elderly or disabled member over the 200% gross test the wrong eligibility answer, shown with a concrete household. They fall in five groups:

- **A federal exclusion or definition the volume does not apply, though its own practice is that 7 CFR 273 overrides the narrower Colorado text (CO-344, CO-119, CO-3704, CO-1382, CO-409 carry `statute_override`)** (SR-1, SR-2, SR-3): one funeral agreement per member is excluded in full under 273.8(e)(2), not up to $1,500, and per member, not per holder; the resources of a member who receives SSI or public assistance are excluded (273.8(e)(17), not implemented anywhere); personal property held as an investment and a revocable burial fund are resources (273.8(c)(2), 4.408.1.B), while OQ-1202 says 273.8(c)(2) is "not on file in full" (it is, twice).
- **Exemptions read wider than the text** (SR-4, SR-5): the home is excluded whether or not anyone lives in it or intends to return; income-producing property and installment contracts are excluded without the "income consistent with its fair market value" test.
- **The $1,500 low-return test and burial spaces on the wrong unit** (SR-6, SR-7): the test is run on the person's total real property, not on each resource at the household's interest; a joint burial space is counted whole on each owner's list; a plot for a relative outside the household is excluded (OQ-1250's assumption against the text).
- **Couples and joint holdings** (SR-8): a holding shared with a spouse in a nursing facility (a separate household) is counted at the person's half, against 4.409.C (OQ-1252's assumption against the text).
- **Time** (SR-9, SR-10): resources are taken once, in the determination month and on the determination date, for every month evaluated: per-month balances, a federal refund's twelve months and a holding given away after the determination month are read in the wrong month, and the resource limit reads the determination month's elderly member while the income tests read the month's (a reader the S4-11 fix missed); and a Colorado EITC paid in the state refund, as the kinds' meanings direct it to be stated, is counted though J.12 excludes it.

There are also 7 P2 findings (SR-11 to SR-17) and 3 P3 findings (SR-18 to SR-20). Open-question classes are in section 4: OQ-1202 (part 2), OQ-1250 and OQ-1252 fail the class check (the text on file settles them; what is missing is a field or a reading), OQ-1251 is mis-recorded ("Not on file: the federal provision behind 4.409.D": 273.8(g) is on file), and four conflicts have no open question at all.
