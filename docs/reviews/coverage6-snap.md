## 1. Verdict

**4 P1 (1 common, 3 rare edge), 3 P2, 1 P3. SNAP does not meet the stopping rule.**

- **C6-1 (P1, common).** When a household has two or more members whose income is prorated (two undocumented parents of citizen children; an undocumented parent and a member past the ABAWD limit; a member refusing an SSN beside an ineligible non-citizen), each prorated member's income, shelter, dependent care and child support are counted at (n-1)/n instead of (n-k)/n. 7 CFR 273.11(c)(2)(ii)-(iii) counts "all but the ineligible members' share". The four-person repro gets $267 where the text gives $387; stored household CO-152 expects $90 where the text gives $304.
- **C6-2 (P1, rare edge).** Categorical eligibility (BCE and ECE) is barred only for an IPV member or a drug felony involving SNAP benefits, as 4.206.C says. 7 CFR 273.2(j)(2)(vii) also bars it when the head of household is disqualified for the work requirements, or when any member is a fleeing felon, a probation or parole violator, or ineligible under 273.11(s). A household with a work-sanctioned head and gross income between 130% and 200% of poverty is eligible for $25 here; by the text it is over the gross income limit.
- **C6-3 (P1, rare edge).** The heating and cooling allowance's energy-assistance route, which needs an elderly or disabled member (Pub. L. 119-21 sec. 10103, CO-1243), still reads that member on the determination month. It is the last S4-11 reader at person scope. A household whose member turns 60 in December, after an October determination, is denied the $616 allowance in December: the engine gives $725 where the text gives $808.
- **C6-4 (P1, rare edge).** No field exists for wages withheld to repay an advance or an earlier overpayment. The text counts only the wages received (4.403.A.3 and 4.405.D.1, both of which name "earned income"). The earnings record (CO-5100) has no such field and its `amount` is defined gross, so the case cannot be stated and the gross pay is counted.

The earlier fixes re-probed here all hold (section 4). The open questions' classes pass the check, with one record to correct (section 5).
