# Audit: same word, different meaning (the income / OQ-396 class)

Read-only audit of the Colorado volume at commit 57663b1 (branch ccr-9d19bf4c-wmnwcl, clean tree; `npm run export -- --volume co`: 1,396 items, of which 517 supplied facts, 280 parameters, 599 derived). Date 2026-11-21. No ledger file was changed.

Binding context (docs/plan-colorado.md, "Product-owner decisions"): "Supplied facts are program-neutral statements of what is true of the person (one immigration status, one income receipt, one relationship). Each program's meaning of a shared word (income, household, caretaker, lawful presence, disability, student, ...) lives in that program's own derived items, never in a second supplied fact." OQ-44 and OQ-396 are "to be fixed in full", and nothing is deferred "until a case needs it".

## Scope and method

1. **Glossary.** Every concept the volume's items read was listed from four places: the supplied-fact identifiers (517), the words in derived-item names (word counts over all 1,396 identifiers, program prefix removed), the relationship roles in `volume.yaml` (`parent`, `child`, `spouse`, `grandparent`, `grandchild`, `caretaker`, `dependent`, `tax_filer`, `tax_dependent`, `buys_prepares_with`, `shares_heat_with`) and the options of the `one of` facts. The brief's word list was covered in full and extended with what the identifiers showed (foster, guardian, sibling, step-, emancipated, Indian, veteran, school-aged, in-kind, lump sum, verification elements).
2. **Readers.** For each concept, the items that carry it and every item that reads them, from `used_by` in `conformance/co/ledger.json`; every `rel`, `exists_related`, `has_relative_in`, `shared_relative` and `reachable` call was listed with its role list (127 calls).
3. **Definitions.** Each reading program's definition was taken from the excerpt its item cites (S-n in `volumes/co/sources.md`). Where no excerpt exists the finding says so.
4. **Evidence.** Where a defect was claimed to change an answer, the claim was checked with `scripts/eval-case.ts`, on the live tree or on a scratch copy of the volume with one case edited (scripts and copy in `/tmp/claude-0/audits/same-word/`).
5. **Not repeated.** docs/reviews/vocabulary-reuse.md (R-1 income, R-2 school enrollment, R-5 disability, R-6 disqualification, R-7 head of household, and the rest) was taken as fixed. This audit re-checks those areas only against the product-owner decision above, which is newer than that review. Where the fix round created a second supplied fact for a program's meaning, the fact is listed again here.

**The relationship-role pass.** The brief says a follow-up pass is adding per-program relationship roles (caretaker, guardian, relative, sibling). As of this writing it has not landed: `volume.yaml` declares the eleven roles above, no commit after 57663b1 touches them, and no worktree under `.claude/worktrees/` declares a new role. This audit therefore audits the current state. Section A gives what that pass has to do to close the relationship findings, and one warning (SW-0). OQ-396's stated blocker ("the engine cannot declare a per-program relationship role from a volume", OQ-132) no longer holds: `volume.yaml` now declares the roles and the engine reads that list (`src/engine/ledger-index.ts`, `buildIndex(items, meta.relationship_roles)`).

**Classes.** (a) one meaning everywhere, correctly shared; (b) different meanings, already split correctly; (c) different meanings sharing one item, a defect; (d) one meaning split into several items, a duplicate. **Priorities**, from the brief: P1 affects an eligibility or benefit answer, or certification (verification status included); P2 maintainability or approval risk; P3 style.

Impact counts are given as *readers / rule tests that state the fact / mentions in `tests/cases.yaml`*.

## 2. Findings

### SW-0. Guidance for the relationship-role pass now in progress (P2)

The decision says "one relationship". Program-prefixed relationship roles (`ma_caretaker_relative`, `cccap_adult_caretaker`, `cw_caretaker`) would put a program's meaning into a supplied fact, which the decision forbids. They would also make a case author state the same real relationship three times. The roles the pass adds should describe the legal or factual relationship only: `stepparent`/`stepchild`, `legal_guardian`/`ward`, `foster_parent`/`foster_child`, `other_relative` (symmetric) and `holds_out_as_spouse_with` (symmetric), as SW-1 to SW-8 propose. "Sibling" should not be a role: it follows from `parent` and `stepparent`, and each program picks which kind of sibling it means (SW-2). "Caretaker" should not remain a role under that name: it is five programs' words for different tests (SW-3). Each program's caretaker is a derived item.

## 3. Proposed convention section for docs/conventions.md

To be added after "Naming and vocabulary" as rules 13 to 18.

> **Words with more than one meaning.** Some words in this volume mean different things in different programs: household, unit, member, parent, child, sibling, caretaker, guardian, relative, spouse, head, dependent, lives with, absent, student, school, disabled, blind, elderly, homeless, institution, resident, income, earned, unearned, countable, resources, assets, shelter, utility, medical expense, work, employment, hours, lump sum, in-kind, month. The volume's glossary (docs/reviews/audit-same-word-different-meaning.md, section 1) lists each program's definition with its excerpt.
>
> 13. **A supplied fact states what happened or what is true, never a program's classification of it.** The neutral fact describes the person, the relationship, the receipt, the holding or the setting in words that need no program's rule to apply. Two exceptions: (i) a finding made outside this volume, recorded as an event, may be a supplied fact with the finding program's prefix (a county's medical certification, a Case Management Agency assessment, a court order, an SSA award, another program's case status under rule 11); (ii) a figure the rule itself takes as attested (the product-owner decision on lighter determinations), named for what it is (`attested_household_income_pct_fpl`).
> 14. **A program's meaning of a shared word is a derived item with that program's prefix**, citing that program's definition. Never a second supplied fact, never a role, and never a stand-in read through `otherwise` from another program's fact.
> 15. **An unprefixed derived item has one meaning.** Its meaning names every program that reads it and cites each reader's definition. A program whose definition differs reads its own prefixed item instead. A shared derived item may combine neutral facts (`age`, `is_colorado_resident`, `is_related_to`, `half_siblings`); it may not stand for "what each program counts" (`has_unearned_income`).
> 16. **Relationship roles name legal or factual relationships only**: parent (legal: natural or adoptive), stepparent, spouse (legally married), holds_out_as_spouse_with, grandparent, legal_guardian, foster_parent, other_relative, has_primary_responsibility_for, tax_filer and tax_dependent (a tax fact), buys_prepares_with, shares_heat_with. Siblings are derived from parent and stepparent, not stated. No role carries a program's term (caretaker relative, adult caretaker, non-parent caretaker, parental control, head of household). Each role's meaning is written in volume.yaml next to its inverse.
> 17. **Co-residence is `lives_with`, never an edge.** A relationship says how two people are related, not where they live. Every household, caretaker and custody item reads `lives_with` and the program's own temporary-absence item.
> 18. **Facts about a home or a household have one value per home** (scope `case`, or a home record), not a value carried on a person chosen by convention.
>
> Naming of the program items: `<prefix><the program's own term>`, using the rule's term rather than the shared word (`cccap_is_adult_caretaker_of`, `ma_is_caretaker_relative_of`, `cw_is_non_parent_caretaker_of`, `snap_is_under_parental_control_of`, `sebt_is_homeless_child`).
>
> **accepts_from.** Rule 3 is unchanged: a program reads another program's prefixed item only when its rule names that program's result, and lists it in `accepts_from`. A neutral fact or a one-meaning shared derived item needs no `accepts_from`. Add a governance warning, `read.shared_definition`: an unprefixed derived item read by a program its meaning does not name. Extend `name.prefix` to supplied facts whose meaning cites one program's rule (the vocabulary review's N-4, declined then; the product-owner decision now requires it).
