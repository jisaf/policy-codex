# Pattern sweep: the cash programs (Adult Financial and Colorado Works)

A systematic sweep of the three patterns in which six rounds of review kept finding wrong answers, over every Adult Financial item (OAP, AND-SO, AND-CS, the Home Care Allowance, burial, disaster assistance and the deeming of a spouse's, a parent's and a sponsor's income; 9 CCR 2503-5, D-80) and every Colorado Works item (9 CCR 2503-6, D-81) in volumes/co/cash, and the parameters, record fields and shared items they read. Started from branch ccr-9d19bf4c-wmnwcl at 14558ff; merged with origin at 950e322 (the MAGI pattern sweep) and again at 5f9c38c (the SNAP, CCCAP, LEAP and SUN Bucks pattern sweep) before the gate. Date: 2026-11-24.

The three patterns (the product owner's brief, after docs/reviews/coverage6-cash.md sections 1 and 8, coverage4-cash.md and the cash parts of field-sweep-snap-cash-nonmagi.md):

1. **Month-by-month timing.** An item evaluated for a month reads facts and ages as they stand in that month (docs/operators.md, Resolution step 7 and "Ages in a month"; the time contract month-v1).
2. **Defaults the rules don't give.** Every `otherwise` and `unknown` guard, every record field's `default` or `when_absent`, every "If not stated" reading declared on a fact, and the `lives_with` fallback of convention rule 17: a cited rule gives it, or the fact is required where read (decision 4 of 2026-11-23).
3. **Couples and household membership.** Spouses (living apart, deemed, dying), sponsors (outside the case, dying, joint), minor parents, caretakers, stepparents, members who die, are born, join or leave, the unit against the household: each composition per month, each count and sum over the right members.

## 1. Method

**The lister.** `scripts/audit/pattern-sweep-cash.ts` walks every derived item whose program is AdultFinancial or ColoradoWorks (258 after the round; 254 at 14558ff) and writes one row per candidate:

- pattern 1: in an item evaluated for a month, every read of a person-, case- or global-scope supplied or derived item that is not a parameter (`month-reads-*`); every `det_date` and `det_month` (`determination-date`); every `at` a month other than the month in context (`at-other-month`); every person- or case-scope cash item a month item reaches, with the dated or time-varying facts it reads (`non-month-in-month-chain`); every record sum that reads no date of its records (`record-sum-undated`);
- pattern 2: every `otherwise` (`otherwise`), every `unknown` guard (`unknown-guard`), every record field with a `default` or `when_absent` that a cash record sum reads (`record-field-default`), every cash reader of `lives_with` or `dwelling_id` (`lives-with`), and every `otherwise` in a shared item a cash item reads (`shared-otherwise`); the facts that declare an "If not stated" reading were listed apart (scratch `declared.ts`, its result in section 4.2);
- pattern 3: every cash item that reads a relationship (`rel`, `exists_related`, `has_relative_in`, `shared_relative`, `reachable`), groups persons (`persons`, `filter`, `count`, `sum`, `exists`, `greatest`, `in_group`, `of`), or reads a spouse, sponsor, death, birth, membership, absence or sanction fact.

At 14558ff it listed **1,424 candidates**: 421 for pattern 1, 889 for pattern 2, 114 for pattern 3. Each was judged against D-80 and D-81 on file; the verdicts are rules in `scripts/audit/pattern-sweep-cash-verdicts.yaml` (the first rule that matches a candidate gives its verdict, and a candidate no rule matches fails the run), and `docs/reviews/pattern-sweep-cash-candidates.md` lists every candidate with its verdict, generated by `npx vite-node scripts/audit/pattern-sweep-cash.ts --verdicts scripts/audit/pattern-sweep-cash-verdicts.yaml --from <the list at 14558ff> --md docs/reviews/pattern-sweep-cash-candidates.md`.

**The probes.** Each defect has a probe with its answer computed by hand from the quoted text: a rule test on the changed item (the hand computation in its title or the comment above it) and, for the end-to-end shapes, a stored household (CO-1030 to CO-1032). The rule tests are the probes where an item-level test shows the whole of the defect.

The classes of "right" (each verdict's text, with the paragraph that fixes the point in time or gives the reading, is in the candidates file):

- **R-HCA** (17; 1; 4): the Home Care Allowance is decided at the approval and paid only from the month after it, with no retroactive payment (3.570.13.F, S3503), so the determination's facts (age five, an absence of thirty-one days, a thirty-day stay) decide every month paid.
- **R-REDET** (12; 3; 8): 3.605.5.C tests child support at the redetermination over the six months before it.
- **R-CURRENT** (4): the transfer penalty divides by "the current Adult Financial Program grant standard" (3.520.76.D.2).
- **R-COUNTY** (9; 3; 41): a finding the rule has the county or a hearing establish, so none stated is none (IPV and EBT findings, failures to apply or cooperate, refusals, rebuttals, good cause, closures and sanctions, the diversion period, the hardship findings, an optional member's request to join, 3.604.2.C.2; a request for disaster assistance, 3.570.52.C).

- **R-RECORD-TIME** (pattern 2, 520): a record read in its month, its stream from its start or its job's start through its end; the absent month, start or end has the meaning the schema declares.
- **R-RECORD-RULE** (pattern 2, 116): a record field's default is the rule's: income and resources count unless an exclusion is shown (9 CCR 2503-6 3.605.3, "Unless otherwise specified, any unearned income is countable"; 9 CCR 2503-5 3.520.781.C, 3.520.786, "Such exempt income is limited to the following"; 3.520.72.A, "Unless otherwise specified, a resource is countable"), a payment is the month's unless shown otherwise (3.520.781.G; 3.605.1.A.2).
- **R-BOUND** (pattern 2, 10): CO-5350 answers from the known parts when they already exceed the resource limit.
- **R-STRUCT** (33; 28): the composition built per month (CO-7303, CO-7304), the person-scope groups answering who may join (coverage pass 6 CA6-4).
- **R-DIVERSION**, **R-DISASTER**, **R-AVERAGE**, **R-STATIC**: a diversion is assessed and agreed at the determination for a one-time payment (3.606.8.A); disaster assistance is determined on the request (3.570.51-52); irregular self-employment is averaged over twelve months read by `sum_months` (3.520.783.F); a fact that does not change within the months.

## 4. Right by the text: the notable judgements

### 4.1 Pattern 1

- **The Home Care Allowance reads the determination date** (age five, thirty-one days out of State, a thirty-day stay): 3.570.13.F pays only from the month after approval, with no retroactive payment, so no month before the determination is paid; kept, cited in CO-1217's precision.
- **Child support at redetermination** (CO-2550 to CO-2553, CO-2842): 3.605.5.C tests the six months before the redetermination; the determination month is the right anchor.
- **The transfer penalty divisor** (CO-3840 at the determination month): 3.520.76.D.2, "the current ... grant standard".
- **Resources** (CO-4412, CO-4722 read the first day of the month, the determination date in its month): 3.510 "Resources", "held as of the first day of a calendar month or as of the date of application".
- **Verification** (105 reads): one value for the determination; 3.520.4-3.520.5, 3.604.3.
- **Diversion** (3.606.8.A) and **disaster assistance** (3.570.51-52): one-time payments assessed at the determination.

### 4.2 Pattern 2: the defaults kept, with the rule that gives them

- County and hearing findings (the PO's carve-out): the IPV and EBT findings, failures to apply or cooperate, refused potential income and resources, rebuttals, good cause, extensions, closures, sanctions, the diversion period, the OAP hardship finding, the self-support and unsaleable exemptions, an optional member's request to join (3.604.2.C.2), a request for disaster assistance (3.570.52.C). Each fact's meaning says "If not stated, ... reads this as ..." (convention rule 12).
- Record fields: the exclusion-establishing defaults (`pass_set_aside`, `claim_expense`, `spent_on_covered_costs`, `for_dependent`, `for_person_living_elsewhere`, `used_for_uncovered_medical_care`, `designated_for_lump_sum_costs`, `foster_care`, `farm`, `temporary_job`, `irregular`, `retroactive`, `income_producing`, `produces_for_household_use`, `used_in_trade_or_business`, `af_found_unsaleable`, `af_found_not_twfc`, `intends_to_return`, `intent_in_writing`, `taxes_on_withdrawal`) are the rule's: income and resources count unless the exclusion is shown (9 CCR 2503-6 3.605.3; 9 CCR 2503-5 3.520.781.C, 3.520.786, 3.520.72.A). The time fields (`month`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `job_start_date`, `job_end_date`, `disposed_date`, `returned_date`, `regular_date`, `share`, `for_person`, `annual_contract_pay`, `taxable_amount`) carry the meaning their `when_absent` declares. The schema files were not edited: their defaults and meanings are shared, and each is the rule's for the cash readers by the paragraphs above.
- cw_unit_eligible_from_date falling back to the application date: 3.609.2.A.1 gives it ("The initial payment to eligible clients shall include assistance beginning with the date of application").

### 4.3 Pattern 3

The Colorado Works unit of the month (coverage pass 6 CA6-4, CA6-5: born by the month's end, alive on its first day, a dependent child, parent, caretaker or expectant parent of the month) now also requires living in the home in the month (PS-T8); the bars on the whole unit for one member (closures, the diversion period, sanctions, child support cooperation) are read over the determination's members, as pass 6 kept them. Adult Financial's couple is the spouse of the month (PS-M1), its sponsors those living (PS-M2); the AND-CS parents those alive in the month (PS-M1); a spouse who lives apart is deemed (3.534, "with no living-together condition", S3204) and, since PS-D7, verified.

## 5. Recorded and listed

- **OQ-1435** (state policy choice, Cash programs policy owner): the day of an earlier month on which an absence or a stay is measured. Assumption: the month's last day (CO-7600; CO-4401). OQ-951 (the day an age is read) and OQ-953 (whether the first day of an absence counts) are the nearest choices. OQ-951 revisited: seven more readers now take the age in the month (PS-T1 to PS-T7).
- **OQ-1436** (state policy choice, Cash programs policy owner): a status the case states for the determination date, read in an earlier month the determination covers (the 79 RES-UNDATED candidates: a strike, a fleeing felon, disqualifications, marriage, emancipation, immigration and sponsorship facts, disability, an SSDI award, SSI as an award in the CW unit's exclusion and the spouse exemption, receipt of Colorado Works or Medicaid, residence, the setting where no entry date is read, school attendance, the unit's head).
