## 1. Verdict

**8 P1 found (1 common, 7 rare edge). The family does not meet the stopping rule.** 4 P2, 1 P3.

Every fix of coverage pass 6, the non-MAGI pattern sweep, the Medicaid status-by-month round and the cross-list round holds (section 4). The new P1s fall in four groups:

- **Figures of a year or of the July adjustment read at the determination (F7-1, F7-2).** The pattern sweep made the facts of each month per month, but its mechanical test did not reach a `[det_month]` used as a date inside `latest_month_of_year` or `as_of`, nor a person- or global-scope item that reads a versioned parameter. The MMMNA standard is the one adjusted in the July before the determination month in every month, so retroactive months before a July (and months after a later July) get the wrong community spouse allowance, family allowance and patient payment; the personal needs allowance, the home maintenance maximum and the home equity limit are the determination year's in a retroactive month of the year before.
- **Couples (F7-3, F7-4).** An HCBS spouse living at home with the community spouse is counted as part of the community spouse (income, shelter, medical needs), which removes the increased CSRA; a legally separated applicant (marital_status `other`) with the spouse outside the case is read as having no spouse, though § 8.100.7.L makes "no exceptions for legal separation".
- **General requirements and income definitions the long-term care path does not read (F7-5, F7-6).** The IMD exclusion of § 8.100.3.G.1.c is not tested by the long-term care, MSP or Buy-In outcomes; and the 300% gross income counts LEAP and federally excluded payments, which § 8.100.5.F.6 exempts for long-term care and federal statutes exclude "for any purpose" (OQ-822's choice cannot reach them).
- **Pathways narrower than the text (F7-7, F7-8).** § 8.100.6.C.10.a (an SSI infant's Medicaid from the date of birth) is not modeled; the Children's Buy-In charges each of two same-age eligible children a premium, against Q.1.g.iii.
