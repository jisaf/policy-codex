## 3. The specific checks asked for

**F7-8, twins sharing the one premium.** The rule text does not settle any order among the children, same-day or not. § 8.100.6.Q.1.g.iii (D-65, line 3087) reads in full: "For households with two or more children eligible for the Medicaid Buy-In Program for Children with Disabilities, the total premium shall be the amount due for one eligible child." It fixes only the household's total. It names no child who bears it, by age or otherwise. Q.1.g.ii ("Premiums are charged beginning the month after determination of eligibility. Any premiums for the months prior to the determination of eligibility will be waived.") is the only other sentence about whose premium is due. Putting the premium on the oldest child is itself the volume's presentation choice, and sharing it equally among same-day children is consistent with the total. The choice holds when both children's premiums are due. It fails when one child's premium is waived for the month (V7-4). Because the text leaves the assignment open, record it with the program owner (an open question or a line in CO-621's precision) and report the household total beside the per-child figures. The unrounded 70 / 3 is V7-9.

**CCCAP's rental_property on every business record (R7-1) against decision 4.** Decision 4 (docs/plan-colorado.md): "Missing information means 'cannot determine' unless the rule says otherwise. Each supplied fact states once whether it is required or what its rule-given default is; readers do not invent defaults." Requiring the flag is consistent with the first sentence: H.4.e gives no default for whether a business is rental property, so where the flag decides the answer, cannot determine is right (C1-D). It breaks the second sentence. The field carries `required: true` and also a meaning that says "CCCAP reads it only on a record whose hours average under twenty a week". Those are two statements of one fact's status, and they disagree. The readers also require more than H.4.e needs: a lone business at or above twenty hours a week is self-employment whatever the flag, yet the engine cannot determine it (C1-F). That is V7-7 (P2: every stored business record states the flag, so no stored answer moves).

**Do the new required facts make common households cannot-determine where the rule gives a default?**

- `for_living_expenses`: no rule default exists (d.ii excludes only what is "used for educational purposes and not for living expenses"). But the field is read where it cannot matter, when the taxable amount is 0, which covers every educational loan and every tuition-only grant. Those households become cannot-determine with no need (V7-3, P1 rare). Where the taxable amount is more than 0, requiring it is right.
- `first_qualified_status_date`: no rule default exists. The engine does not ask for it from an LPR admitted directly (M4-D) or one whose status held now is five years old. It does ask from every LPR inside five years whose earlier status is `other`, including the common adjustment from a non-qualified visa, where the value is qualified_status_date again (V7-8, P2). No wrong answer.
- `marital_status` for Adult Financial: no rule default exists. 3.520.63.A has the county determine the client's marital status, and af_is_married asked for it since coverage pass 6 (CA6-7: CO-3829's split $20 already read it for every client). The new reader (CO-7690) adds no new cannot-determine household: CA2-B, C, E answer **$452** once the status is stated, and only CA2-D (not stated) is unknown. Holds.
- The separation dates (CO-7352, CO-7506, CO-7670, and the new moved_into_home_date CO-7671): 273.10(a)(1)(i) gives no default, and OQ-1450 declares the reading for the determination month and after. moved_into_home_date has a declared default. The separation dates are asked only for a month before the determination month and only for persons joined by an edge, which keeps the ordinary case (everyone lives together) free of them. Two gaps remain: a pair that never lived together cannot state any value (V7-1, P1 rare), and one move must be stated twice for a stepchild (V7-5, P2).
