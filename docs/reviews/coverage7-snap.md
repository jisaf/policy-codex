## 1. Verdict

**4 P1 (0 common, 4 rare edge), 2 P2, 2 P3. SNAP does not meet the stopping rule.** All four P1s are rare edges. Three of them (C7-1 to C7-3) appear only when the application month comes before the determination month and the household's circumstances changed in between. That gap is ordinary (a household applies late in a month and is determined in the next), but a change inside it is uncommon. C7-4 is a sibling the three-pattern sweep's T-11 missed.

- **C7-1 (P1, rare edge).** A member who moved out after the application month, or during it, is left out of the application month's household. SNAP builds every month's household from `lives_with` (one `dwelling_id` per case, on the determination date). It does not read the dated separation facts the volume already has (`ceased_living_with_spouse_date` CO-7352 and `ceased_living_with_parent_or_child_date` CO-7506, which only Medicaid reads). No fact dates when a member joined. 7 CFR 273.10(a)(1)(i) determines the application month on "the household's circumstances for the entire month of application". MOVE1: the engine gives **$217** for October where the text gives **$280**.
- **C7-2 (P1, rare edge).** The energy-assistance route to the Heating and Cooling Utility Allowance (CO-1243, fixed by T-10) has an upper bound and no lower bound. A LEAP payment made after the month being evaluated gives that month the $616 allowance, though 273.9(d)(6)(iii)(D)(3) counts a payment only "in the current month or in the immediately preceding 12 months". LIHEAP-LATE: the engine gives **$312** for October where the text gives **$280**.
- **C7-3 (P1, rare edge).** The 90-day stay in another person's home (CO-037, fixed by T-4) counts days from `temporary_residence_start_date` to the month's first day. A stay that began after the month gives a negative count, so the household reads as homeless in a month before the stay began. STAY-LATE2: the engine gives **$222** for October where the text gives **$198**.
- **C7-4 (P1, rare edge).** The exclusion of a child student's earnings (CO-2569, reading CO-2568) ignores the expected completion of the program. T-11 added that check to the ineligible-student rule and the work registration exemption, but not here. A 17-year-old who graduates in December keeps her earnings excluded in January. 273.9(c)(7) and 4.405.C exclude only the earnings of a student who is attending school, and continue the exclusion through a break only "provided the child's enrollment will resume". GRAD17: the engine gives **$725** for January where the text gives **$509**.

The C6 fixes and the three-pattern sweep's SNAP fixes all hold where they were re-probed or re-read (section 4). The open questions' classes pass the check (section 5). One unrecorded reading (C7-6) should become an open question, or be settled by the C7-1 fix.
