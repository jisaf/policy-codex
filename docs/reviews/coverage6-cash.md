## 1. Verdict

**7 P1** (3 common, 4 rare), **5 P2**, **3 P3**. Neither cash family meets the completion bar.

The P1s fall in three classes:

- **A month's facts read from the determination date** (CA6-3, CA6-4, CA6-5): Colorado Works has no pregnancy in a month and counts a child not yet born as a dependent child of the month before the birth (common: a pregnant applicant who gives birth while the application is pending); the unit's size, standards and minor-parent deeming are taken from "a dependent child in some month" and from the age on the determination date, so a month on the other side of a birthday is built with the wrong unit (rare); a member who died stays in the unit (rare). Coverage pass 4's CA4-15 fix made the dependent-child test per month but not the composition, the standards or the minor-parent items that read it.
- **Unstated facts read as a value no rule gives** (CA6-1, CA6-2, CA6-7): an Adult Financial applicant whose eligibility date is not stated gets the full grant for the application month and every month before it, though the application date is stated (common); a Colorado Works determination that does not say it is a redetermination is read as an application, so consistent child support under $500 is counted and a recipient is denied (common); twenty-odd applicant circumstances (pregnancy in a unit with no child, a sponsor's household size, a strike, a spouse's Medicaid, an absence abroad, ...) are read as false or 0 (rare in effect). Coverage pass 4's CA4-16 removed fifteen such defaults; these remain.
- **A missing pathway** (CA6-6): a one-time diversion payment is refused to a client who declines to assign child support, which 3.606.8.A.6 does not require of diversion clients (rare).

Every P1 was reproduced with the engine and its answer computed by hand from the quoted text (section 2 and section 3). The fixes of the earlier passes hold except where a finding here names the gap a fix left (section 5). Every open question the cash items cite is a recorded state policy choice, a "needs Legal" question (which docs/reviews/open-question-status.md counts as a policy choice) or blocked on a named document (section 6).
