# 26 U.S.C. 111 — Recovery of tax benefit items, subsection (a)

26 U.S.C. 111(a). https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/111

Retrieved 2026-11-23 from the source above (Legal Information Institute) and converted to plain text without editorial change; subsection (a) only. Excerpts in sources.md quote this file.

(a) Deductions

Gross income does not include income attributable to the recovery during the taxable year of any amount deducted in any prior taxable year to the extent such amount did not reduce the amount of tax imposed by this chapter.
