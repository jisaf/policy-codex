# HCPF Operational Memo OM 25-078 — 2026 Increase to Resource Limits, Medicare Savings Programs and Low-Income Subsidy, effective January 1, 2026

Colorado Department of Health Care Policy and Financing, HCPF OM 25-078 (issued Dec. 29, 2025; effective Jan. 1, 2026 through Jan. 1, 2027), https://hcpf.colorado.gov/sites/hcpf/files/HCPF%20OM%2025-078%202026%20Increase%20to%20Resource%20Limits%20-%20Medicare%20Savings%20Programs%20and%20Low-Income%20Subsidy.pdf, as captured by the Internet Archive on 2026-01-17 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260117110647/...). Retrieved 2026-09-18; text extracted from the PDF (the tables are column-major in the extraction). States the QMB/SLMB/QI-1 resource limits: 2025 $11,160 individual / $17,470 couple; 2026 $11,450 / $17,910; QDWI $4,000 / $6,000 both years; LIS 2025 $17,600 / $35,130, 2026 $18,090 / $36,100; the MSP limits include $1,500 (individual) / $3,000 (couple) burial expenses.

Purpose and Audience: 

The purpose of this memo is to advise Eligibility Sites (county departments of 
human/social service), Medical Assistance (MA) Sites, and Eligibility Application 
Partners (EAP)) (referred to in this memo as “agency” or “agencies”) of the increased 
resource limits for Qualified Medicare Beneficiaries (QMB), Specified Low-Income 
Medicare Beneficiaries (SLMB), Qualified Individuals (QI-1), and Low-Income Subsidy 
(LIS) resource limits. 

Information:
