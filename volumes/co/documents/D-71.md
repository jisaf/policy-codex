Information:  

When one spouse resides in an institution, such as a nursing facility, Home and 
Community Based Services (HCBS), or the Program of All Inclusive Care for the Elderly 
(PACE), a portion of the couple’s combined income and resources is protected for the 
spouse living in the community to help prevent spousal impoverishment. The 
Community Spouse Minimum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance (MMMNA) and the 
Shelter Allowance are used to determine the amount of the institutionalized spouse’s 
income that may be allocated to the community spouse to meet monthly living 
expenses. 

The standard maintenance allowance for the community spouse is based on the 
Federal Poverty Level (FPL) and is adjusted annually on July 1. 

The MMMNA is set at 150 percent (150%) of the FPL for a family of two.  

The Shelter Allowance is equal to 30 percent (30%) of the approved MMMNA and 
is also adjusted based on changes to the FPL. 

Action To Be Taken:

guidelines as of July 1, 2026, when making an eligibility determination for LTC.
